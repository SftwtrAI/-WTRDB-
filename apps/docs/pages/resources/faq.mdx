# FAQ

## Is wtrdb a graph database?

wtrdb is the platform around a graph database: it handles source-to-graph extraction, governance, access control, visualization, integrations, and operations. FalkorDB is the graph store used by the application.

## Can I query a graph from an AI agent?

Yes. Publish a Brain Endpoint and connect an MCP-aware client to its Streamable HTTP URL. See [Brain Endpoints](/integrations/brain-endpoints).

## Does raw Cypher need to be enabled?

No. It is optional per Brain Endpoint. Leave it disabled unless the integration needs direct graph queries.

## Does compliance tooling make us certified?

No. It supports control assessment, evidence collection, reporting, and export; your organization remains responsible for certification and compliance decisions.
